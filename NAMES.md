## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. TendHQ — my #1 pick
- **Domain options:** `tendhq.com`, `tendhq.app`, `tend.mom` (newer TLD)
- **Meaning:** "Tend" = to care for (kids, business, home, self). "HQ" = her command center.
- **Why it works:** Short (5 letters visible), strong business feel, double-meaning for both nurturing and running. Easy to say on TikTok: "Get TendHQ, link in bio."
- **Handles:** `@tendhq` on IG, TikTok, FB
- **Not surfaced in search** → likely available. Verify before buying.

### 2. HandsFullApp — emotional resonance
- **Domain options:** `handsfullapp.com`, `handsfull.app`, `gothandsfull.com`
- **Meaning:** Every target user **is** hands full. This captures the pitch perfectly: "We built this for moms with their hands full."
- **Why it works:** The name sells the app. When a tired mom sees it, she nods. Also memorable without being twee.
- **Handles:** `@handsfullapp` or `@gothandsfull`
- **Not surfaced in search** → likely available.

### 3. TheBrainForHer — brand lore play
- **Domain options:** `thebrainforher.com`, `brainforher.app`
- **Meaning:** "The Brain" is already the name of the AI inside the app — this compounds the lore so every feature you add to the Brain reinforces the brand.
- **Why it works:** Positions AI as THE feature, not a side-thing. Future product line (ForHim, ForTeens) writes itself.
- **Handles:** `@thebrainforher`
- **Not surfaced in search** → likely available.

## How to verify + buy (30 seconds)

1. Open **https://cloudflare.com/products/registrar/** — cheapest at wholesale, no upsells
   (Alternative: Namecheap, Porkbun, GoDaddy)
2. Type the domain in the search box
3. If available, buy it. Expect:
   - `.com` → $10–12/yr
   - `.app` → $14–18/yr (HTTPS is automatic on `.app`)
   - `.mom` → $30–40/yr (premium TLD)
4. **Also secure the same handle on:**
   - Instagram (https://instagram.com/accounts/signup/)
   - TikTok (https://tiktok.com/signup)
   - Facebook Page (https://facebook.com/pages/create)
   - Preferably Twitter/X (free)
5. Claim a Cloudflare Pages site with the domain — routes to `realryannichols.github.io/amanda/` or wherever the app lives.
