Questions guys actually ask

Will I see all her data?

No. She checks boxes for what you can see: calendar, baby year, pregnancy visits, kids roster, me-time summary. Her Brain conversations, prayer journal, vent-mode transcripts, financial details, and safety flags are always private. Always.

Do I have to be into "apps"?

No. The Together view is one calendar + one baby-year timeline + a place to write her a letter. Five taps a week. That's the bar.

What if we're not married?

App doesn't care. "Partner" means whoever she says. Husband, boyfriend, fiancé, co-parent, significant other — same seat, same permissions, same respect.

Does the crisis alert feature monitor her conversations?

Not unless she opts in on a specific flag. And even then: you only get a "she's having a rough time, maybe reach out" ping. You never see what she wrote. Ever. The content stays hers. See our privacy page for the full flow.

How do I cancel?

One tap in her Settings or yours. No confirmation calls. No guilt-trip retention flow. Gone.
