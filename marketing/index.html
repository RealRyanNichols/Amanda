Questions other moms asked.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device first. You can optionally back it up to our encrypted cloud. We never sell your data. We never train AI on your data. You can export everything as JSON any time.

What if I cancel?

Your data stays on your device. You still have your letters, your bookmarks, your photos. Cancel any time from Settings — no phone calls, no guilt trips.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After first load, everything works without a signal. Great for flights, hospital stays, the drive to grandma's.

Is this a medical / financial / legal tool?

No. It's a gentle helper for tracking what matters to you. For medical, legal, or tax decisions — please talk to a professional.

Who is this for?

Moms. Expectant moms. Mom business owners. Families who want one place for their calendar, kids, faith, and plans. Faith-friendly and mom-first — but you don't need to be religious to use it. The Bible + prayer features are there if you want them; hidden if you don't.

Is the Bible really free?

Yes. Every translation, every verse, forever — paid or free tier, on day one or year ten. "We are never, ever going to hold God's word hostage over money." That's a permanent promise baked into the code.

I'm not pregnant. Do I still need this?

Absolutely. Pregnancy features only show if you tell us you're expecting. The core app is for every mom: calendar, kids' schedules, meal planning, gratitude, finances if you run a business, a voice-powered AI friend to talk to. Pregnancy is one module among many.

Who built this?

A man named Ryan built it for the woman he loves — an actual mom, business owner, and faith-led woman who was carrying too much in too many apps. She loved it, so we made it for you too.
