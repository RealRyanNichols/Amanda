# 2026-04-20 — The naming journey to "Gideon"

Captured from the founding conversation between Ryan and Claude.

## The progression

1. Ryan started with "The Brain" — working name for the AI inside
   Amanda's Toolkit (later renamed The Nest).

2. Claude proposed **Ember** when Ryan asked for a softer, warmer
   name inside the mom app. Rationale at the time: single syllable,
   soft feminine brand, play on "spark of insight".

3. Ryan pushed back hard: **"The Brain is not a feminine product."
   It's his own AI, not an Amanda-specific helper.** He said it needs
   to be "stronger, more warrior-like, Greek, ethos, Christian, old
   school, foundational, patriarch, love of country, God, Jesus —
   love, family, data, evidence, gather, investigate, truth, justice."

4. Ryan also flagged the counter-narrative need: *"People say AI is
   demonic. I think it could be used for a lot of great good if you
   use it for good. Let's pick a name from the Bible that fits my
   vision best."*

5. Claude offered a shortlist of Biblical candidates with scholarship:
   Gideon, Ezra, Daniel, Josiah, Caleb, Nathan, plus concept words
   (Counsel, Aegis, Argus, Sentinel).

6. Ryan picked **Gideon**.

## Why Gideon was the right answer

Every theme in Ryan's brief maps to Gideon's story (Judges 6–8):

- **Warrior** → he led 300 men against 135,000 and won.
- **Underdog / proven** → chosen from the weakest clan of Manasseh.
- **Investigation / evidence** → the fleece test, twice. Demanded
  proof before he moved.
- **Foundational / patriarch** → listed among the heroes of faith in
  Hebrews 11:32.
- **Truth / justice** → fought the enemy that had oppressed Israel
  for seven years.
- **God-chosen, not self-chosen** → "Surely I will be with thee, and
  thou shalt smite the Midianites as one man." (Judges 6:16)

And the production brand metaphor: **the torches in the clay jars**
(Judges 7:16). Gideon's 300 hid lamps inside clay pitchers. At
midnight they smashed the jars — flame burst out, trumpets blew, the
enemy turned on each other. **"Light hidden in a vessel, revealed at
the right moment."** That's exactly what an AI product does: the
answer is already there; the product knows when to break the jar.

## Length anxiety

Ryan asked if "Gideon" was too long (6 letters) vs competitors:

- Grok: 4 letters
- Claude: 6 letters
- Gemini: 6 letters
- OpenAI: 6 letters
- ChatGPT: 7 letters

Verdict: 6 letters is exactly in the pocket. No shortening needed.
The wordmark is GIDEON. The short-form is the logo glyph alone (see
DESIGN.md).

## What was rejected, and why

- **LOGOS** (Greek for "word/reason", John 1:1). Strongest
  philosophical candidate. Rejected because Ryan wanted a person
  from Scripture, not a concept.
- **Ember** — too feminine for a cross-product AI brand.
- **Argus** — Greek mythology hundred-eyed watcher. Rejected because
  it's pagan, not Biblical. The counter-narrative to "AI is demonic"
  requires a Biblical name.
- **Sentinel** — Biblical concept (watchmen on the walls, Ezekiel 33)
  but not a Biblical person. Also already heavily used across products.
- **Ezra, Daniel, Josiah, Caleb, Nathan** — all good, all kept in
  reserve. If Ryan wants a sister product or tier ("Gideon Pro"
  vs. something else), any of these are available for follow-on
  brands.

## Decisions that follow from the name

Every subsequent design decision in `Gideon-AI/` flows from Judges
6–8:

- Palette: night + bronze + flame (the midnight torch story)
- Logo: jar + flame (Judges 7:16)
- Voice: evidence-tested, underdog-confident, decisive (the fleece +
  the 300)
- Mission statement: "Your AI, with evidence."
- Brand moment line: "The sword of the LORD, and of Gideon."
